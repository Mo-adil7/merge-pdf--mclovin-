Convert PDF to PowerPoint online

Use MergePDF to turn a PDF into a PowerPoint PPTX file in your browser. Each PDF page is placed on its own PowerPoint slide while keeping the page appearance intact.

How it works

  1. Select a PDF file.
  2. Click Convert to PPTX.
  3. Wait while the PDF pages are rendered into slides.
  4. Download the generated PowerPoint presentation.

Important note

This converter creates slides from rendered PDF pages, so the visual layout is preserved but the text and objects on the slides are not individually editable like a native PowerPoint document.

Privacy

Your PDF is processed in your browser. The site does not require an account or an upload server.

🔒 Your PDF stays on your device. No account or upload required.
